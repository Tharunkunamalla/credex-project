# Prompts — StackSpend

## Product prompts used in the build

- Summary generation prompt for the audit report: concise executive summary, 3 to 4 sentences, no bullets, focused on savings and actionability.
- Lead email prompt: brief transactional follow-up with audit link, savings recap, and Credex call to action.

## Landing page positioning prompt

- Communicate a fast, free AI spend audit for startup teams.
- Emphasize clear savings, deterministic logic, and no login required.

## Internal note

- Keep prompts short and specific because the product depends on clear, defensible outputs rather than creative writing.
