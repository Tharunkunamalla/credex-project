# Go-To-Market — StackSpend

## Target audience

- Startup founders.
- Engineering managers.
- Ops and finance leaders who want to reduce AI tooling spend.

## Primary hook

- A free, fast audit that finds overspend in AI subscriptions, licenses, and API usage.

## Distribution

- Shareable audit results that can be forwarded internally.
- Credex brand association for discounted AI credits.
- Lightweight content marketing around AI spend waste and SaaS optimization.

## Conversion path

1. User completes audit.
2. User sees savings report immediately.
3. User leaves email to receive the full report.
4. Follow-up email drives consultation or discounted credit conversation.
