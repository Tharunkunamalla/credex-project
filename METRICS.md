# Metrics — StackSpend

## Core product metrics

- Audit completion rate.
- Email capture rate after report view.
- Share rate for audit result links.
- Average monthly savings shown per audit.
- Conversion rate from audit to follow-up lead.

## Quality metrics

- Build success rate in CI.
- Test pass rate.
- Time to generate an audit result.
- Error rate for `/api/audit` and `/api/lead`.

## Business metrics

- Number of audits per week.
- Qualified leads per week.
- Credex referral or partner conversions.
